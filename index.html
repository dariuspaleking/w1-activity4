The Butterfly Life Cycle

Egg

The butterfly starts as a tiny egg laid on a plant leaf. The egg is usually very small and can be different colors depending on the butterfly species.

Larva (Caterpillar)

The caterpillar hatches and spends most of its time eating leaves. It grows quickly and sheds its skin several times as it gets bigger.

Pupa (Chrysalis)

The caterpillar forms a protective chrysalis where it transforms. Inside the chrysalis, amazing changes happen as the caterpillar becomes a butterfly.

Adult Butterfly

The adult butterfly emerges from the chrysalis with wings and can fly. Adult butterflies drink nectar from flowers and lay eggs to start the life cycle all over again.
